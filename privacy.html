Privacy policy

Yatra Mitra — Darshan Booking Assistant · Updated 24 October 2026

Publisher: Yatra Mitra
Contact: support.yatra.mitra@gmail.com

Information you provide

The extension handles devotee names, ages or dates of birth, gender, identity-document types and numbers, booking contact email, city, state, country and postal/PIN code, group membership, chosen dates and hours, ticket quantities, optional laddu quantities and donation amounts. It also stores booking drafts, preferences and automation status.

Where information goes

Saved information is stored in this browser's extension local storage. This version has no developer-operated server, cloud sync, analytics or advertising. When you start automation, relevant information is entered into the official TTD website at ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in so its booking forms can be processed. This means booking data is shared with TTD, even though the saved library is local. TTD and its payment providers apply their own privacy policies.

Payment and authentication

You sign in to TTD yourself. The extension does not ask for or store your TTD password, OTP or payment-card credentials. It may advance to the payment page in the mode you select; you complete payment manually.

Storage and deletion

Records remain locally until removed. Deleting a devotee removes them from the active library, groups and prepared drafts, but a local undo snapshot remains and may persist beyond its five-minute undo window until overwritten or cleared. An existing run may contain its own data snapshot. Use the extension's clear-data action to remove saved booking data, groups, devotees, undo data and run state. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage. This does not delete data already submitted to TTD.

Security

Saved data is not encrypted by the extension. Password-style ID masking only conceals the display; it does not encrypt the stored value. Use a trusted device and browser profile. The extension's website access is limited to the official HTTPS TTD domain.

Your choices

You choose which people to save and include, and start or stop automation yourself. Only enter other people's information when you have their permission. You can edit or delete records and clear saved data.

Future changes

This release is free to use; TTD charges are separate. Any future subscription or new data handling will be disclosed before use, with this policy updated as appropriate. No subscription billing is included in this version.

Independent service

This extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by TTD or any government agency. For privacy questions contact the publisher above; do not send government ID numbers in support messages.
